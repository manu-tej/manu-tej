### Manu Tej Sharma Arrojwala

I am a computational biologist and engineer. I am curious about how diseases can be cured.

I have spent almost seven years at **Arcus Biosciences**, an oncology and inflammation biotech (tumor-immune phenotyping, neoantigen and TCR workflows, multi-omics platforms and pipelines).

On the side I have been building and evaluating research agents and writing up what I find. Each project below has its status on its page.

**Threads**
- [coscientist](https://manu-tej.github.io/threads/coscientist/): an experimental multi-agent system that generates, critiques and revises scientific hypotheses, with six specialist roles and an Elo tournament that ranks the candidates. Paused; source private.
- [ai-scientists and benchmake](https://manu-tej.github.io/threads/ai-scientists/): evaluations of coding agents on BiomniBench-DA biology tasks, with task variants, repeated runs, scoring and failure analysis, and a tool to author and validate benchmark task bundles. Active. Benchmake authoring continues locally; evaluation runs have been on hold since October 2026. Source private.
- [dogma and methods-graph](https://manu-tej.github.io/threads/dogma/): dogma treats a hypothesis as a graph of claims and checks whether a proposed analysis has the data, method assumptions and provenance to assess each one; methods-graph connects bioinformatics methods to their assumptions, diagnostics and data handoffs. Paused; [dogma](https://github.com/manu-tej/dogma) is public, methods-graph private.
